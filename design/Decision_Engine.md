# Decision Engine

## Purpose

The Decision Engine is the central orchestration layer of Property Intelligence.

It evaluates incoming information and determines the next best action.

The Decision Engine does not perform AI analysis itself.

Instead, it coordinates:

- AI Engine
- Database
- Buyer Matching
- Opportunity Scoring
- Workflow Automation
- Notifications

## Inputs

Property

Buyer

Seller

Market

Offer

Communication

AI Analysis

## Outputs

Tasks

Recommendations

Scores

Notifications

Automation Events
