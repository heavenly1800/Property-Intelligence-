# Property Intelligence Architecture

## Core Principle

Everything in the system revolves around relationships between entities.

The application does not simply store data—it continuously analyzes relationships, detects opportunities, and recommends actions.

## Primary Entities

- Property
- Person
- Company
- Deal
- Offer
- Market
- AI Analysis
- Conversation
- Task
- Document

## Guiding Philosophy

Every new feature should answer at least one of these questions:

1. What do we know?
2. What changed?
3. What should happen next?
4. Who should be notified?
5. What action creates the most value?
