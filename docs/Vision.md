# Property Intelligence

## Mission

Build an AI-powered acquisition platform that helps investors identify, analyze, negotiate, and close real estate opportunities across multiple investment strategies.

## Vision

Property Intelligence is an intelligent acquisition platform that matches motivated sellers with qualified buyers while automating research, valuation, communication, and deal management.

Unlike traditional CRMs, Property Intelligence actively assists users by recommending actions, prioritizing opportunities, and generating insights using AI.

## Target Users

- Land wholesalers
- Residential wholesalers
- Commercial wholesalers
- Buy & Hold investors
- Fix & Flip investors
- Developers
- Realtors
