# Assignment Strategy Playbook

## Objective

Purchase a property at a sufficient discount and assign the purchase contract to another investor for a profit.

---

# Positive Signals

- Strong buyer demand
- Rapidly growing market
- Utilities available
- Legal road access
- Clear title
- Long-term ownership
- Equity likely
- Desirable zoning
- Buildable lot
- Nearby comparable sales
- Active investor purchases
- Low holding costs
- No environmental concerns

---

# Negative Signals

- Flood zone
- Wetlands
- Landlocked parcel
- Steep terrain
- Poor access
- Low buyer demand
- Title concerns
- HOA restrictions
- Declining market
- Unbuildable lot

---

# Deal Breakers

- No legal access
- Unresolvable title issues
- Environmental contamination
- No realistic buyer demand

---

# Questions the Decision Engine Should Answer

1. Is this assignable?
2. Why?
3. What risks exist?
4. What information is still missing?
5. What should happen next?

---

# Possible Next Actions

- Call seller immediately
- Generate offer
- Order title search
- Verify legal access
- Research utilities
- Skip property
